### Why the RCT exclusion is sufficient

For fixed contact numberings and a trigger schedule, the observed ciphertext determines every offset. Each known plaintext letter requires one equation W(x)=y. A permutation wiring exists exactly when identical inputs demand identical outputs and distinct inputs demand distinct outputs. Any consistent injective partial map extends to a permutation of 26 contacts. This is necessary and sufficient, independent of language scores and annealing recovery.

Dropping a mistaken published plaintext constraint is valid because plaintext does not drive the state. Dropping a ciphertext constraint does not repair altered downstream state. A free initial offset can be absorbed into the arbitrary wiring; fixing the initial offset to zero omits no such freedom.

For F26/F26L, every registered setting fixes its transformation. Independent enumeration covers all listed alphabets, block/offset settings, final short blocks, and the 36 outer choices plus no outer stage. It does not depend on a blind-solver shortlist.

## 4. Corrections

### A. The final scorer sees a shortlist

`f26_solve.py` decrypts all 139,120 settings and scores them with quadgrams, then returns the global **top 64**. `run89.py` applies the final order-six letter model to those 64. RCT similarly uses its fixed heuristic selection and polishing procedure. The final maximum is over pipeline outputs, not every mathematical setting under the final scorer.

Affected claims occur in the preregistration scoring/decision rules, `RESULT.md`, the review of PR #98, runner/evaluator docstrings and L178. “Enumeration cannot miss” describes visiting all finite F26 settings with the first scorer; it does not guarantee final-score recovery. `best_by_arm` contains only subfamilies surviving the global shortlist, not necessarily each subfamily's own best.

The identical deterministic pipeline was applied to all shuffles, so its saved-score Monte Carlo comparison remains interpretable. The separate exact checker is exhaustive and unaffected. No newly ranked result has been substituted into the frozen output.

### B. RCT passed a weaker key requirement than the earlier proposal

The delivered ChatGPT bounds required at least 42/48 with the right method **and key, or a formally proven equivalent**. Claude's actual preregistration instead requires correct arm, confidence and at least 95/97 letters; key recovery is descriptive. This change preceded the holdout. It is a design deviation needing clearer disclosure, not a threshold changed after observing results.

RCT `setting_right = 46/48` checks contact numberings and trigger schedule but omits secret wiring. Full wiring equality is **37/48**; allowing arbitrary completions of unused contacts gives **39/48**. H_121, H_150 and H_194 count as successes at 96, 96 and 95 letters while differing on used contacts. Thus RCT would fail the earlier 42-key requirement even under that permissive interpretation.

Agreement on one message's visited contacts is weaker than global cipher equivalence. A different completion can act differently on another message. Future gates must specify the intended equivalence. Actual plaintext-recovery GO stands; stronger key recovery was not demonstrated. The mathematical exclusion requires neither recovery claim.

### C. A single F26 ciphertext substitution can affect two letters

The preregistration, exact-check docstring and result say the >=23/24 DROP screen generally covers one ciphertext-symbol error. That is false. After undoing the pointwise outer substitution, a middle symbol supplies a row to one plaintext position and a column to another. A compatible setting can therefore show only **22/24** matches. A >=22 screen is necessary for this error model, not sufficient to establish a specific valid correction.

A synthetic counterexample is included: plaintext 97 A's, AZ, block 5, u=1, v=2, no outer stage. Changing ciphertext position zero from A to O changes decoded positions 1 and 2. Synthetic known positions 0-23 yield 22 matches, so the old screen rejects the true setting.

**The R89 exclusion survives.** Its maximum is only 7. Correcting any one fixed-length ciphertext symbol can improve a fixed setting by at most two known letters, giving a global upper bound of **9/24**. Independent enumeration also finds no original setting at or above 22. This proof does not cover insertion, deletion or changed block alignment. RCT's one-removed check covers wrong published plaintext letters only; ciphertext corruption remains outside its exclusion.

### D. Fifteen original alphabet classes were replaced

R89's catalog shares only **AZ, KRYPTOS, PALIMPSEST, ABSCISSA and CANDLE** with the original development kit under the documented row/column relabeling symmetry. Both lists contain 20 distinct classes. The original ORCHARD, MYSTERY, TRAVEL, MECHANIC, RIVER, PHILOSOPHY, HORIZON, GARDEN, SECRET, TEMPLE, MARKET, TUNNEL, LIBRARY, MIRROR and CIPHER classes were not included.

The changed list is explicitly preregistered and defines a valid finite experiment. Its exclusion cannot be extended to the omitted catalog or the entire F26 construction. “All offsets” also means exactly u,v in 1..b-1 with u unequal to v; zero offsets were not searched. This audit did not test omitted settings.

### E. Resource semantics and CI

The original R89 workflow directly invoked auxiliary K4 witness, evaluation and exact checks despite the mandatory wrapper rule. This review wraps future replays and preserves their logs. It does not invent historical logs.

The unchanged historical wrapper sets an address-space limit **per process** and a wall timeout. Its `ru_maxrss` is not aggregate simultaneous worker memory; it enforces neither aggregate CPU time nor CPU count. “4 GB per run” and the reported approximately 1.27 GB RSS must not be interpreted as verified aggregate four-worker memory. No aggregate overrun was established. Before another experiment, choose and enforce aggregate resource semantics explicitly. The old pinned wrapper is not silently rewritten.

### F. Smaller corrections

- Six RCT misses occurred, not the five listed: 94, 82, 7, 90, 94 and 6 letters.
- F26L H_116's true text/key is saved, but scores below the confidence cut and is outranked by an abstaining RCT result. It is a failure under the joint decision rule.
- “A single carving error would hide an RCT solution” is too certain. Some changes alter trigger history; others do not. Three failed diagnostics do not estimate a general failure probability.
- 201*(5+3) is **1,608**, not 2,010. The larger registered cap is conservative, so the typo does not show an overrun.
- [R82 PR #77](https://github.com/tylerj-spec/kryptos-k4/pull/77), head `1550736de568abdf442686e4a15b8889c585f2f3`, already supplied the general 2x13 construction, equivalence/marginal tests, an exploratory fixed-chart screen and an outer-stage proposal. R89 adds a distinct finite catalog and formal search/exclusion. “2x13 fractionation has not been tested before” is too broad; the prior work does not make R89 redundant.
- Exact consistency is independent of language statistics, conditional on published letters and cipher specification. Changing the language outside known positions cannot rescue an inconsistent setting. Other mechanisms, preprocessing and alignments remain open.

## 5. Next-round decision and limits

Close the precisely tested R89 families as **EXACT_SCOPE_EXCL**. Retain the blind result as **STAT_NO_SIGNAL** for the actual pipeline. Keep the 15 omitted original alphabet classes and other untested domains open; omission is not positive evidence for them.

Before another blind round, preregister the actual catalog, final score, retention rule, key-equivalence definition and aggregate resource budget. Use fresh controls; the revealed R89 holdout is now development data. Do not reinterpret or repeatedly rerun it to pass a revised threshold. Do not rerun an already excluded finite family merely to improve optimizer recovery.

The pinned R59 letter model is untracked. Its hash and build sources exist, but this audit did not obtain/rebuild the exact model, independently recompute its letter scores, repeat blind optimization or repeat the recorded 8/8 determinism probes. Compiler/CPU/NumPy versions were not frozen with the original run. These limits concern blind recovery; the separate mathematical exclusion does not need the model.
