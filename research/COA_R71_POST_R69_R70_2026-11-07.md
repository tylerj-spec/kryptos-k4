The next useful question is a *cipher-method diagnosis*, not a new key list: can one physically plausible, reversible construction generate **both of K4's ciphertext-only fingerprints** (width-7 doubled-letter alignment and width-21 repeated vertical pairs), while preserving the observed low index of coincidence?

The novel work proposed for this repository is their JOINT calibration against **matched structured-cipher controls and mechanism-specific synthetic generators**, rather than only same-letter shuffles. This is a mechanism gate, not a claim that widths 7 or 21 describe the true key.

## 2. Independent research and what is actually new

**Primary academic analysis:** Richard Bean, [Cryptodiagnosis of Kryptos K4](https://doi.org/10.3384/ecp183153), HistoCrypt 2021, sections 2.1, 2.3–2.5 ([PDF](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/153/109/52)). In the unmodified 97-letter ciphertext arranged at width 21, 76 vertical neighbor pairs include **11 extra repeated bigrams** (AZ, BS, IT, KK, LS, LW, PK, QZ, SN, WA, ZT). A letter-preserving permutation gives at least that count at this *chosen width* approximately 1/6750 times. Bean also discusses an even-base Gromark 5-digit recurrence with a length-21 regularity, but neither that nor his plaintext-derived arguments proves K4 uses Gromark.

**Second fingerprint:** 6 adjacent doubled-letter starts; **5 lie in one column modulo 7**, when aligned to the carved sequence. Published ciphertext-only count, independently rechecked from the original K4 ciphertext in this review.

**External replication warning:** [Alexis Rondeau, experiment 0056](https://alexisrondeau.me/k4/exp/0056-width-7-and-21-fingerprints-look-elsewhere/) reports look-elsewhere corrected p about 0.010 for the width-21 repeats, p about 0.005 for the column concentration (0.018 conditional on six doublets) over widths 2–48 against 10,000 shuffles. [Research notebook](https://alexisrondeau.me/k4/notebook/) describes further family simulations: ciphertext autokey at lag 21 creates the first fingerprint more often but none of its tested recipes created both; later work tested combinations. **These are third-party findings, not independent new tests in this repository.** Many of its later attacks use published crib consistency and must NOT be imported into an R53 blind solver.

**First-party construction context:** [Ed Scheidt, WIRED 2005 interview](https://www.wired.com/2005/01/inside-info-on-kryptos-codes/) said the fourth process masked access to English-language statistics, and that the technique might be unfamiliar. [Sanborn, WIRED 2005 interview](https://www.wired.com/2005/01/questions-for-kryptos-creator/) described modifying Scheidt's techniques and using visual/matrix systems, and said earlier texts contained instructions referring to later ones. These facts justify investigating process-level composition, but **do not prove** a 7/21 grid, Dutch/German disguise, or any specific cipher.

**Novelty claim limited to our repo:** A search of current docs/CIPHER_TYPES_TESTED.md, LEDGER.md and docs/CRYPTANALYSIS_METHODS.md found no dedicated calibrated test of the **JOINT** 7/21 fingerprint against the six R67-like structured cipher families and explicit matched mechanism simulators. Claude must grep ALL code before treating R71 as actually new.

## 3. Proposed R71: a bounded, preregistered method-discrimination experiment

**Question:** Can a specified class of *mechanisms*, with unknown keys and fresh ordinary-English source messages, produce both ciphertext features jointly substantially more often than carefully matched wrong-family ciphers? If no class can do so with adequate statistical power, retire this target signature as a practical solver guide until new construction evidence appears.

**No plaintext candidate generation at R71.** R71 is entirely ciphertext-side cryptodiagnosis. An eventual R72 would recover unknown keys and plaintext blind on synthetic plants first. The K4 published plaintext must not enter R71 in any way.

### A. Deterministic features (97 unaltered A–Z letters)

For i = 0..95, let d_i = 1(C[i] == C[i+1]); D = sum d_i. For width w and residue a, n_{w,a} = sum over doubled-letter starts i with (i mod w == a). Define A_w = max_a n_{w,a}. For K4, D = 6 and A_7 = 5.

For lag w, let count_w(xy) be the number of i in 0..96-w such that (C[i], C[i+w]) is the ordered pair xy. Define R_w = sum_xy max(0, count_w(xy)-1). For K4, R_21 = 11 over 76 pairs. Separately compute the usual IC and number of distinct letters.

**Crucial:** These particular widths and thresholds were selected after historical inspection of K4. An uncorrected single-width p-value is NOT confirmatory. The R71 null applies the same *whole width-selection procedure* to every control (e.g. w=2..48) and evaluates the joint maximum, with appropriate standardization for different numbers of pairs per width. Report the fixed width-7/21 rates as descriptive and the scan-corrected statistic as primary. Do not multiply two marginal p-values as though they were independent.

### B. Generative families (exact finite definitions to freeze BEFORE any actual run)

Start with SIX arms, explicitly reusing preexisting, published and historically plausible generators where possible:
1. OTP/long unrepeated independent key (negative baseline).
2. Plain running-key/long-key Vigenere family (negative baseline).
3. Quagmire III and periodic mixed-alphabet family (R67-like structured baseline, not another full K4 search).
4. Homophonic substitution plus documented transposition (R66/R67-like baseline).
5. Gromark-like linear recurrences: base 2 and 10, 5-position state, with full output alphabet (explains why Bean raised width 21; **not** a direct Gromark K4 search).
6. Ciphertext-feedback/autokey with lag selected from {1, 7, 21}, and a *separately specified* optional 7-phase key-advance/reset operator (model-design arm, not claimed historical). The direct CTAK cases have been tested/excluded under R68's specified models; the purpose here is to measure generator fingerprints, not re-run those exclusions.

Before preregistration is declared frozen, Claude must define exact invertible encryption and decryption pseudocode for every arm, ranges, parameter sampling distribution, and whether any alternative arm is statistically redundant. Limit composition depth to TWO nontrivial stages so models remain distinguishable. If the optional 7-phase operation cannot be defined without arbitrary fitting, REMOVE that subarm rather than invent it to make K4 appear exceptional.

**Selection provenance:** The 7/21 mechanism family itself comes from already-seen K4 ciphertext patterns; do not call the family prediction independent confirmation. Favor published, historically attestable rules and include complexity penalties.

### C. Controls / multiple-testing

- Every generator produces independent length-97 ciphertext from **hidden**, freshly selected English plaintext and independent hidden keys/state. Sources and random seeds disjoint from R65/R67/R69/R70 development and held-out controls where practical. Do not reuse the 192 R67 controls as fresh evidence.
- Do an initial *power pilot on synthetic ciphertext only* to validate that the program can distinguish a deliberately known 21-lag source from ordinary sources without seeing any hidden truth. If not, label mechanism diagnosis INCONCLUSIVE; do not interpret the K4 statistic.
- Suggested preregistered definitive sample: 20,000 independent encryptions per arm, and 100,000 fresh letter-count-preserving shuffles for null calibration, deterministic per-sample RNG and recoverable chunk checkpoints. The exact count may be adjusted by Claude in the pre-registration **before** the first target run, with a clear compute cap and acceptance limits. Report sample/rejection counts and uncertainty per arm.
- The model's output distribution must be reported both **unmatched** and **after matching on ciphertext-only nuisances** (length 97, all 26 letters, and IC near K4's 0.03608 within a fixed tolerance). Record matching acceptance rates, especially where extreme-flatness matching is rare, to avoid artificially favoring one family.
- For every control and K4, compare the same scan over widths w=2..48 and the SAME fixed process for ranking width pairs. Compute the **joint** empirical tail probability and its confidence interval within each family; correct for six family comparisons (e.g. a single global maximum over the family comparisons or a predeclared family-wise correction). Keep the original shuffled baseline separate from structured generators rather than assuming one pooled null is universally exchangeable.
- **Power gate:** pre-register a target ≥80% mechanism-classification sensitivity on held-out synthetic cases where the joint feature is present, with ≤1% false positive identification on held-out wrong-family controls. If too few joint-feature cases occur to estimate sensitivity (suggested minimum 30 positive cases per relevant arm), mark LOW_POWER and do not run K4 method selection.
- **Decision gate:** A class is PROMISING only if (i) the power gate passes, (ii) its joint-feature enrichment versus the matched wrong-family baseline is ≥10× with a 95% confidence interval lower bound >3×, (iii) its discrimination survives whole-width look-elsewhere correction and family-wise correction at p≤0.01, and (iv) it has a small explicit invertible definition. These are proposed bars subject to Claude's synthetic-only power review before freezing.
- **Stop:** If no family passes, ABSTAIN and ask for new *independent* construction evidence; DO NOT expand families or nudge thresholds based on the K4 outcomes.

## 6. Summary of why this changes the direction

R69 tested *which carved route should be privileged*, and R70 tested *which preselected book might be the key* by a blind ranking (negative) and a separately labelled crib-assisted consistency audit (also negative but **not R53-blind evidence**). Both are negative within their stated scopes. R71 instead asks which **class of reversible construction** can explain two *cryptographic fingerprints* visible in the original ciphertext at once, and requires the class to reproduce the phenomenon on unseen **synthetic messages** before any attempt to solve K4. This respects the user's method-first direction, the limited 97-character evidence, and the strict R53 blind boundary.
