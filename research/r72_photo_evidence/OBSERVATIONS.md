## Source hierarchy and consequential caveats

PBS's original transcript dates NOVA scienceNOW to **July 24, 2007**. Kryptools' catalog associates the stills with **July 1, 2007**; unresolved date discrepancy. Source images are television screenshots of a historical worksheet, not first-generation artist originals.

The **K3 exact transformation** and **14 × 31 arithmetic** are independent controls. They do not demonstrate K4 had a filled 98-cell sheet. An earlier review of PR #60 recorded a hypothetical overlap of ragged-grid routes; the revised script creates 26,701 route permutations, but proof of prior K4 execution depends on actual experiment logs, not just tuple equality to a generated superset.

## Falsifiability checklist

**TOP #4:** Reframe video while sliding sheet: if the lettering stays fixed with the plastic sleeve when paper changes, reject the hypothesis that it labels a separate K4 paper sheet. If the lettering moves with paper, still do not assume K4 encryption until a separate operator is shown.

**Folds:** Without a full reading-order map before and after a fold, do not claim a reversible cipher operation.

**14 × 31 source:** An independently transcribed full source image must be compared with the final carved text; any mismatches must be documented as source evidence, not automatically corrected.

**R73:** No family/key search based on width-7/21 anomalies or published known plaintext. Only test a separately documented new invertible procedure, after synthetic hidden-power controls and preregistration.
