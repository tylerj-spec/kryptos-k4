## 0. Binding safety rule — R53 strict blind freeze

Read [r53/BLIND_RULES.md](../r53/BLIND_RULES.md) first. The K4 candidate generator, key search, route selection, language scorer, candidate ranking, and parameters must receive **no published K4 plaintext fragments, no known plaintext positions, no unreleased archive plaintext, and no clues derived from them**. Read K4 exclusively from the repo's original ciphertext fixture. This research note is for **human hypothesis design** and must not be mounted in the restricted blind-run environment.

**Sequence:** (a) independent source and mechanism justification; (b) preregister methods and normalization; (c) run hidden synthetic controls to establish true-message recovery and false-positive rate; (d) run the locked solver against unaltered K4 and appropriate controls; (e) write full raw outputs, parameters, exact forward re-encryption checks, hashes, and an immutable attempt ledger; (f) only AFTER freeze allow a separate evaluator to consult the published K4 letters, as necessary but not sufficient validation. A postfreeze match never authorizes retraining or selection on that feedback. No external checker, undisclosed-plaintext retrieval, K5 issue activation, or merge without owner's explicit instruction.

**Correction to the proposed 'fast exact key check':** Enumerating book offsets and selecting those reproducing the 24 public K4 letters is **crib-assisted key search, prohibited by R53**. It cannot be substituted for a blind run. Even if a source and offset match every published letter, that alone is not an independently solved plaintext or key. Historical finite-family consistency audits may be preserved as explicitly **post-hoc/crib-assisted**, never as independent candidates. No source should be selected BECAUSE it contains an already published plaintext clue; disclose any possibility of such selection bias.

## 4. Specific next test recommendation (after evaluating R67)

**R69 preferred: worksheet/fabrication mapping feasibility and synthetic power**, not another broad ciphertext key sweep.

- **Question:** Can a small set of faithfully reconstructed K3 worksheet coordinate transformations and documented row/copy conventions explain a measurable structural property in independently generated 97-character ciphertexts, and do those maps supply better blind recovery power than arbitrary routes?
- **Evidence gate (before coding):** Obtain the original 2007 NOVA frame, K3 Step 1 and Step 2 image, and 14×31 panel image; have two independent transcribers verify grid axes, directions, fill order, separator handling and ambiguous cells. Each candidate transform must point to a *specific visible mark or documented K3 step*. Explicitly exclude any transform constructed to fit K4 public plaintext positions.
- **Positive controls:** Reproduce known K3 transposition stages exactly, using K3's already solved text solely as a CONTROL (never using published K4 plaintext). Generate at least 24 novel 97-letter English plants with hidden random keys, masks, and a spectrum of row-filling and directional transforms; require strict held-out recovery of plaintext, route and correct key family where identifiable. Include negative controls such as plausible alternative grid shapes and 97-letter unstructured messages.
- **Nulls:** At least 100 independent controls from structured 97-letter ciphers with matched index of coincidence and alphabet coverage, plus 100 frequency-preserving shuffles. Avoid judging an anomalous K4 route against shuffles alone. Lock selection/statistics before the K4 run and calibrate *maximum score across all route/algorithm variants* per control, not each test in isolation. Report confidence intervals and detection power, not only p-values.
- **Execution:** Reuse existing strictblind runner, source attestations and sealed verifier. In any K4 blind stage mount only original ciphertext, frozen route/map definitions, generic language model, binaries and independent sources; do NOT mount this research report, K4 clue files or postfreeze evaluator. Compare the same pre-registered scoring statistic across all controls and K4, freeze complete output files with source/model/code hashes and deterministic seeds. The K4 reveal/evaluator process is strictly separate and last.
- **Stop condition:** If reconstructing the worksheet yields no unambiguous historically supported new transform beyond R61/R68, **do not launch R69 code**; instead produce a negative research finding and transition to reversible masking test design. If held-out recovery gate fails, do not interpret K4 as negative evidence about the proposed mechanism.
- **Candidate threshold:** A coherent full decode that is exceptional versus the null *max-score distribution*, exactly re-encrypts and survives untouched postfreeze validation merits independent review; it is **not an automatic solution**.

**R70 second priority: explicit reversible English-statistics masking models.** Design a short finite, invertible masking family with *predeclared rules* and measured recovery on hidden plants first. Examples worth engineering only if motivated by 1980s techniques: interrupted keyword streams, alphabet or key-state resets on independent physical delimiters, variable-length reversible phonetic spelling with a fully specified dictionary and length handling, or deterministic symbol/category encoding. Do not use German/Dutch words from public clues to tune these rules. English phonetic disguise is a *hypothesis*, not a supported historical fact.

**Named-source running-key pilot (lower priority):** Once raw source editions are secured, define 11 previously frozen paths, the exact R68 M1/M2 definitions and 12 conventions, and a finite list of strictly independent normalization variants. Enumerate offsets, compute complete decrypted outputs, score English language *without seeing any K4 clue*, and retain a fixed top-K per source/family/convention. Calibrate the global maximum over all offsets/sources/paths/conventions using holdout messages encrypted from actual, withheld source offsets plus frequency-matched structured nulls. Freeze every returned candidate and its source/offset/hash before any public-letter check. A high language score from a long novel's arbitrary window can be accidental. If source selection was driven by a disclosed clue, label the whole exploration as non-independent and do not elevate matching outputs to blind evidence.
