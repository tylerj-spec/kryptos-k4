## Decision

**Run both ideas as distinct, separately approved and preregistered studies, with R69 first.** The row-direction experiment is a narrow diagnostic of an existing K4-specific anomaly, whereas the exact-edition book scan has a low historical prior but can generate candidates under narrowly specified running-key methods. Keep a third *reversible spelling/masking* concept separate: the rejection of German-language **key text** does not reject German/Dutch-influenced spelling of an English plaintext.

Evidence: `r65/RESULT.md` reports, under the frozen German-key model, K4 SNAKE_L = -320.8593, carved order ~-357.3, SNAKE_R ~-360.5, and the next route -349.3572. `r67/RESULT.md` on PR #50 reports no gap exceedance among 192 IC-matched structured controls (maximum 25.5157 vs K4 gap 28.4979), and 21/24 planted positive detections. R65b's high-power postfreeze key-fragment check rejects its specific *ordinary German running-key* interpretation statistically, not mathematically. `r68/RESULT.md` finds no K4 candidate across the classic keyed-path families tested. Thus the R69 question is **where a misspecified scorer's preference comes from**, not whether we have identified K4's transposition.

## R69: proposed fixed protocol (must be implemented, reviewed, hashed and committed BEFORE a run)

1. **Input and 16 routes:** The sole K4 target is the repo's original unchanged 97-letter ciphertext fixture. Split the *carved order* into consecutive 0-based segments `[0,4)`, `[4,35)`, `[35,66)`, `[66,97)`. For each 4-bit combination of F/R, reverse the indicated segment *internally* and concatenate the four segments in that order. Use exactly the R61 convention `CT[j] = X[perm[j]]` and `r64.unroute` inversion. Include FFFF (carved), FRFR (SNAKE_L) and RFRF (SNAKE_R); byte-check these three against historical R61 routes and include permutation/inverse property tests. The 4-letter first segment is included to avoid an unreported assumption, but underpowered for attribution.
2. **Instrumentation:** Identical R65 stage-1 GERMAN pipeline (R63 solver binary, English/German LMs, word table, beam 15000, TOPN 2000, LAMBDA 1, no polish, all six conventions). Hash every dependency including source, executable, models, route generator and source fixture; do not retrain or tune on K4. State clearly that this is a diagnostic score from an already disfavored model, **not evidence for German key text**. Do not add plaintext anchors, clue positions or prior crib-derived settings to the blind stage.
3. **Power gate BEFORE any new K4 scoring:** New, hidden-original synthetic English + held-out German-key controls; 32 planted 97-letter examples, exactly two per route, independently forward-encrypted. Lock generation seed, disjoint source bytes, SHA-256s, independent encryption verifier and withheld-truth digest in the completed preregistration. Stage and score them in a no-network, read-only, nonroot blind environment. Freeze complete raw output and SHA manifest before separately unsealing truth. Gate: at least 24/32 exact recoveries of **rows 2–4's direction bits**, with results also by pattern and for row 1. If failed: R69 is INCONCLUSIVE, no K4 run, no post-hoc tuning.
4. **K4 nulls if gate passes:** 300 *fresh* histogram-preserving shuffles (not any previous R64/R65 samples), with fixed unused seed and explicit uniqueness checks. Score also the *previously frozen* 192 R67 structured-control ciphertexts, **clearly labeled reused and IC-selected**, with their original hashes verified. Treat each null population independently; do **not pool** shuffled and structured cases into an exchangeable p-value. Prefer an additional fresh, preregistered structured holdout for genuinely independent confirmation if resources allow. Report each of six R67 cipher families separately, not just the pooled 192; 32/family cannot establish family-specific p < 0.01.
5. **Locked contrasts:** Let `S(b1,b2,b3,b4)` be the maximum of the six convention totals for each bit route. Prespecify signed main effects `D_r = mean(S | b_r=R) - mean(S | b_r=F)` for r=2,3,4. Prespecify `I_24 = mean(S|R2,R4)-mean(S|R2,F4)-mean(S|F2,R4)+mean(S|F2,F4)`, averaging over bits 1 and 3. Compare **absolute** contrast magnitudes to each null's own corresponding contrasts (avoid sign selection after viewing K4). Also report `max_b S(b)`, best-minus-second-best route score, the winning bit pattern, and the full 16-score vector; calibrate max statistics against the **max over all 16** for each null, never an uncorrected single-route null. All four contrast tests must be multiplicity-adjusted (e.g. Bonferroni 0.0125 for familywise 0.05), and all null populations reported separately. Do not call descriptive Monte Carlo exceedance rates a fresh independent discovery: the route-model hypothesis was selected using K4 in R64/R65/R67.
6. **Order and immutability:** (a) implementation + tests; (b) exact protocol, executable/model hashes and fresh controls frozen/committed before revealing truth; (c) hidden synthetic gate; (d) *only if pass*, blind K4+null run; (e) freeze/push every raw score/witness and input/output/protocol digest; (f) separate evaluation with no candidate-generation feedback. Report an exact independent forward transform check for every route record. No published K4 clue strings or offsets in any generator, ranker, stage, null-prep or route selection.
7. **Interpretation / stopping:** A pattern unusually favored under *both separately reported* null populations is **only a lead to prioritize**, not proof of a K4 reading order. **Do not prune every other route from the next cipher-search stage** on this evidence alone; retain carved order, the leading patterns and a systematic negative baseline until independent source/mechanism evidence supports exclusion. If R69 null comparisons are ordinary, retire this specific R65 German-scorer row-direction lead, not the possibility of other transpositions or masked English. R69 produces *no K4 plaintext candidates by design*.

**Estimated scale:** 16 routes x (32 plants + 1 K4 + 300 shuffles + 192 reused structured controls) = 8,400 route-solver calls. The historical ~7-hour wall estimate is conditional on identical hardware and model runtime, **not a commitment**. Any fresh structured holdout adds calls and requires separately estimated capacity.

### Forward-looking masking note
A German-key LMs' failure says nothing definitive about a reversible Dutch/German-like *orthographic transform applied to English plaintext*. To test that hypothesis, first explicitly define a reversible character/word-level grammar based on independent historical/linguistic evidence, verify round-trip integrity and recovery on fresh hidden synthetic messages, and only then submit K4 ciphertext to the locked blinded solver. Avoid free-form changes inspired by already published K4 anchors.
