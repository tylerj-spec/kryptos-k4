# Round 33 preregistration: ciphertext-only keyword columnar plus running/numeric key

Created before examining R33 ciphertext outputs. Round 32's full-family results and known clues are not used by the blind module. No git pushes.

## Hypothesis and scope

Classical irregular columnar transposition keyed by three *pre-K4* Kryptos keywords: KRYPTOS, PALIMPSEST, and ABSCISSA, followed or preceded by fixed-alphabet additive or Beaufort substitution. Retain 97 letters, irregular final row, normal or reversed fill, optional boustrophedon fill, ascending or descending key-sorted column read, top or bottom column read, and forward/inverse route. Exact route file is generated by `blind/col_routes.py`.

- G5 family: all three keywords and all 32 route variants, each with two cipher-stage orders; 192 route-order rows; 100,000 5-digit chain-addition primers, PA and CA in {AZ,KRYPTOS}, three cipher operators.
- CARTER family: the three keywords, four canonical column-read choices (fill left-to-right, no boustrophedon, forward direction), two stage orders; 24 route-order rows; Carter 1923 book letters, both text directions, two key numberings, every consecutive 97-letter window, same 12 cipher conventions.

The 24 clue letters are NOT accessible to `blind/col_routes.py`, `blind/scan_fixed.cpp`, or the scan command. Quadgram ranks complete 97-letter output; no word repairs or positional insertions. Output TSV files and ciphertext are SHA-256 frozen before any program with the known clues is invoked.

## Precommitted positive controls

Two 97-letter natural-language samples from the existing calibration set are encrypted outside the blind module using R33 routes; one with a G5 key and one with a Carter window. Their plaintexts and parameters are stored separately under sealed/. The *identical full family* is then run on each planted ciphertext. A control passes only if the exact original plaintext is ranked #1.

## Decision

A K4 candidate requires a full frozen 97-letter plaintext at both exact known phrases (24/24), independent forward re-encryption and a coherent explanation. Otherwise the tested fixed algorithms are excluded as the actual exact cipher (but not other possible routes/keys). The sealed whole-family matcher checks every setting only after freezing, to guard against top-100 filtering.

No claims of statistical significance from multiplying the binomial(24,1/26) tail by count of settings; outputs are correlated.
