## The diagnostics identify more than a coupled-search problem

The known-block conditions are separate from unknown-two-wiring recovery.

| Conditional diagnostic | >=95 recovery | Maximum matches | Median matches | Truth scores above found text | Wrong found text scores above truth |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| True U, A | 0/48 | 65 | 49 | 48/48 | 0/48 |
| True U, B | 0/48 | 94 | 51.5 | 42/48 | 6/48 |
| True V, A | 0/48 | 72 | 43 | 48/48 | 0/48 |
| True V, B | 0/48 | 93 | 45.5 | 45/48 | 3/48 |

Even with one entire true wiring supplied, the higher-order refinement has no
case at the registered recovery threshold. On 87 of the 96 B conditions, a
known feasible point—the true plaintext—has a better score than the found
point. That demonstrates remaining conditional optimization gaps. It does not
prove every miss is solely an optimization problem.

The independent scalar rescore of **all 31,200 legal one-transposition
neighbors** reproduces the recorded result: **39/48 true keys have a different
feasible plaintext in that neighborhood with a higher order-four score**.
Every recorded best witness re-encrypts correctly and has the claimed score.
This disproves exact-truth global optimality under this objective on those
spent cases. The bounded checker records the score counterexamples without
separately stratifying their match counts.

The coordinator then directly compared each of the 39 frozen best-neighbor
texts with the public truth, without rerunning the scorer or any optimizer:
**25 have fewer than 95 matching letters and 14 have at least 95**, with a
range of 90..96. This separate threshold recount is recorded in
`audit/rotor_followup/NEIGHBOR_THRESHOLD_RECOUNT.json`; it is not a field or an
additional run of the bounded checker above. Thus at least 25 cases have a
recorded higher-scoring local alternative outside the recovery threshold.
This still does not determine the global optimizer's output or prove an
information-theoretic limit. The remaining nine cases without a recorded
higher-scoring neighbor do not establish global optimality either.

The next design therefore must not assume that only coupled search remains.
Conditional optimization and objective adequacy both need evidence. These
diagnostics supply no confidence calibration, false-positive rate, fresh
generalization result or authorization to run K4.
