**Version 3** is version 1's joint annealer with the objective replaced by the R59 letter model truncated at order 5
(`kernel/anneal5.c`, `solve_v3.py`, `dev/run_v3.py`).
- The kernel's scores equal `core.Scorer`'s order-5 score of the explicit decrypt (`dev/check_v3.py`).
- **Parameters:** 8 restarts × 4,500,000 proposals per plant, T0 4.0, then a greedy polish.
- **Final choice:** the 8 best distinct decodes are rescored by the full order-6 model, and top-1 is its maximum.
- The proposal count was set from the measured speed (1.32 million proposals per CPU second) to fit the version
  budget. Nothing else was tuned.
- It used the same 48-plant panel as before: spent books only, no K4 and no clue.

## Diagnosis (`DIAG.json`): the objective is now right; the search is not
- **Objective.** The true plaintext's order-5 score is above the best decode found in **47/48** plants: median gap
  10.6 log10, range −21.0 to +1.1. Version 2's quadgram search found decodes above the truth in 16/48. So the
  stronger objective removes nearly all of the overfitting.
- **Search.** The annealer never gets near the truth's score, at about 36 million proposals per plant.
- **Order-6 model:** the truths score −0.56 to −1.08 per letter, the found top-1 decodes −1.33 to −1.44.
