## Diagnosis (`DIAG.json`, development truth)
**The quadgram objective overfits.** In **16/48** plants the search found decodes scoring **above the true
plaintext** by quadgrams. Every cell has at least one.
- In the other 32, the search did not reach the truth's quadgram score: the median gap is −7.8 log10.
- So both the objective and the search fail.

**The letter model separates truth from these decodes by a wide margin:**
- the true plaintexts score −0.56 to −1.08 per letter;
- the found top-1 decodes score −1.26 to −1.41.

So a stronger objective is necessary. It is not shown to be sufficient.
