## What remains, and what it needs
**The plan's remaining construction work is S3b.** It is a type C (solver development) task:

- first shown on fresh synthetic controls;
- with a registered work order and Tyler's approval, as for R96 and R97.

**Hard parts to plan for:**
- **Error propagation.** An autokey carries any wrong rank forward along its chain, so local search over the
  alphabets has a rugged landscape. That is the same failure mode R97 found for RX2-O.
- **Identifiability at 97 letters.** It must be shown before any K4 contact (charter §6).
