practice messages. A alternated exact conditional unigram assignments; B used
whole-permutation and cycle proposals scored under the actual order-four
objective. Both used the same eight starts per case. Their maximum final-state
letter agreements were 23/97 and 19/97, respectively.

The diagnostic result is stronger than an unqualified search failure:

- Supplying true U still gives B zero recoveries at >=95, with maximum 94.
- Supplying true V also gives zero, with maximum 93.
- Truth scores above the found B state in 42/48 and 45/48 cases, respectively,
  demonstrating concrete conditional optimization gaps.
- A higher-scoring wrong single-swap neighbor exists in 39/48 cases. The
  independently verified finite panel contains all 31,200 legal neighbors.
- A separate direct comparison of the 39 saved best-neighbor texts with truth

Thus both scoring and conditional search need improvement. R94 does not prove
that English is absent, that the two-rotor family is impossible, that a global
optimum has been reached, or that only the interaction between two unknown
wirings remains problematic. Its order-four objective is also different from
the historical v3 order-five objective, whose unavailable full model was not
rerun here.

opened. All 999 new saved states fit the declared retention ceiling. The
algorithms reached their finite no-improvement stops; the resource ceiling
