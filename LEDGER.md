"8 variants" = 4 pairings of AZ/KRYPTOS × additive or Beaufort. "16 pairs" = all ordered pairs of the 4 alphabets.
Status types: **EXCL** = exact contradiction · **SCORE** = compatible but fails a specified English screen · **STAT** = statistically disfavored · **WEAK** = compatible with ≤ 3 checks · **OPEN** = untested or unconstrained · **FEAS** = a complete cipher witness exists (not a language solution).
Language caveat: SCORE/STAT depend on a stated text population. They are not EXCL. Exact results still assume the stated raw anchors, transformation order, alphabets and transcription.
