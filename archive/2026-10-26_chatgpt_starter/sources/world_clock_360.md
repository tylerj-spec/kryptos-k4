# World Clock: 360° face map (built 25 Oct 2026 from photos Tyler supplied)

Read the drum left to right as you face it. The UTC offset increases left to right; the hour ring moves right to left, i.e. clockwise seen from above.

Each face has an **upper list** (above the hour ring) and a **lower list** (below it). Names are as engraved and listed top to bottom.

Confidence tags:
- **R** = read clearly
- **r** = read from a small or blurred image
- **?** = partly legible; check
- **—** = not yet seen

## Three-column view (top text | hour number | bottom text)
**How to read the hour numbers.**
- The ring turns once a day, so no face has a fixed number. Neighbouring faces always differ by 1, and only the starting point moves with the time of day.
- This table uses "Berlin = 12". To match a photo, add the same shift to every row.
- Example: Tyler's photos with JAKUTSK = 24 and EDMONTON = 8 were both taken at Berlin ≈ 16 (+4 to every row).
The hour ring turns once a day, so the number column shows each face's hour **when it is 12:00 in Berlin (standard time)**. Faces run left to right around the drum; the same face spans UTC −12 and +12 (the date line).

## What the comparison shows
- **The 1997 rework was large, not cosmetic:**
  - Names were renamed, added, removed (ALGIER?) and moved between faces: KIEW, NOWOSIBIRSK, SINGAPUR (+7 → +8), REYKJAVIK and MADEIRA (−1 → 0), ATHEN (upper → lower).
  - Spellings changed: TOKIO → TOKYO, PNOM PENH → PHNOM PENH, DJAKARTA → JAKARTA, DACCA → DHAKA, KARATSCHI → KARACHI, DELHI → NEW DELHI.
  - List orders were re-laid out.
  - **Any key built from today's clock would be built from the wrong text for 1989.**
- **The plates also changed before 1997.** "Mexiko City" became "Mexiko Stadt" (press report), and the +10 face (Wladiwostok/Chabarowsk, and the Australian lower list) apparently changed between 1972 and 1984.
- **Coverage of the pre-1997 clock:**
  - Upper lists are known for 15 of 24 faces (−1 to +12; −3/−2 illegible).
  - Lower lists are known for 7 faces, plus a partial +12.
  - Nothing is known for −12 to −4.

## Orientation: which way each face points (25 Oct 2026; orientation.py)
- **Method.** In the 1972 photo (Bundesarchiv L0328-0018), the clock's axis sits about 13–22° left of the TV tower, depending on the lens.
  - The TV tower lies at bearing 262.6° from the clock, 262 m away (coordinates: clock 52.52112 N, 13.41326 E; tower 52.520817 N, 13.409419 E).
  - The seam between faces +8 and +9 points straight at the camera.
- **Result** (35 mm lens estimate; ±5–8°):

| Faces | Points |
|---|---|
| UTC +1 (Berlin) | about 177° (south) |
| UTC 0 | SSW |
| UTC −5 (New York/Washington) | west, toward the TV tower |
| UTC +7 | east |
| +12/−11 (date line) | about north |
| **+8 (IRKUTSK, ULAN BATOR / PEKING, SHANGHAI or 1972 HANOI, MANILA)** | **72° (ENE = 67.5°)** |
| +9 | 57° |

- **Cross-check.** In the 2015 photo the Berlin face is seen head-on with Galeria and the Park Inn tower behind it, so the camera faces north and the Berlin face faces south.
- **The alignment looks deliberate:** the Berlin face faces south (toward the noon sun) and the date line faces north.
- **To verify,** stand at the clock in Google Street View, turn to compass heading 67.5° (ENE), and see which face is in front.
- **Street View cross-check (Tyler, Google Maps, Philipp Klöckner, Sep 2018).**
  - What the photo shows:
    - The Park Inn tower (north of the clock) is behind the clock on the left, and the TV tower (west-southwest of the clock) is not in view, so the camera looks roughly north-northeast.
    - The faces in front are about −1/0: a one-line top and bottom (AZOREN / KAP VERDE), then the long 0-face lists.
    - The ring shows about 17–18 on those faces, i.e. about 7 pm summer time.
    - Shadows are long and fall to the right of the view.
  - In September at about 7 pm the sun is low in the west (azimuth about 265–275°), so shadows point east. With the camera facing north-northeast, east is to the right, which matches the photo.
  - With the orientation a quarter turn off, the TV tower would be in view and the shadows would fall the other way. This confirms the orientation to within about one face; the screenshot is too low-resolution to settle the exact face.

## What lies east-northeast of the clock (25 Oct 2026; results/r12_ene_bearings.log)
Great-circle bearings from the clock (52.52112 N, 13.41326 E):
- **Moscow (Red Square): 67.5°, exactly ENE, 1,609 km.** The rhumb-line (Mercator map) bearing is about 79°.
- Also inside the ENE sector (56.25–78.75°): Vilnius 68°, Minsk 75°, and locally the Volkspark Friedrichshain (62°, 1.7 km).
- The former **Lenin monument** at Leninplatz (19 m, stood 1970–1991): about 78°, 1.3 km (approximate coordinates).
- The Stasi remand prison at **Hohenschönhausen**: about 69°, 6.3 km (approximate coordinates).
- Caveat: a 22.5° compass sector always contains something. Moscow at exactly 67.5° is the one that stands out.
- **Use these as candidate crib words, not as evidence:** MOSCOW/MOSKAU, KREMLIN, LENIN, STASI.

## Is the clock on the line from CIA HQ to KGB HQ? (25 Oct 2026; results/r12_cia_kgb_line.log)
| Line from CIA HQ (Langley) | To Lubyanka (KGB HQ) | To Yasenevo (KGB foreign intelligence, now SVR) |
