## Important warning
The accessible raw OCR contains severe transcription errors, especially front matter and unusual typography. OCR substitutions, missing words, extra headings/page numbers, and chapter transitions can shift every subsequent running-key alignment. Do not mistake this for a reliable diplomatic text. Record retrieval URL, time, SHA-256, edition, normalization method, and uncertain spans. Compare the opening and key sections against page images. Keep original OCR untouched and derive separately versioned A-Z-only streams; never silently repair source text during key evaluation.
