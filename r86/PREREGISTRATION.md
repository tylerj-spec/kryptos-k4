## Model and scope

AZ contact in/out alphabet, arbitrary bijection W over 26 contacts. Zero-based position i, step n in {1,2,3,4}, direction d in {-1,+1}; s_i = d*floor(i/n) (mod 26). Encipher c_i=(W[(p_i+s_i) mod 26]-s_i) mod 26; decipher with invW and opposite shifts. Phase absorbed into W. Exactly eight (n,d) configurations, each over 26! possible wirings; annealing is heuristic and NOT exhaustive. This is a restriction of the design in r85/DESIGN.md. Does NOT exclude n>=5, different alphabets, irregular/multiple rotors, transpositions, masked or non-English, any other cipher. No exact cryptographic exclusions are claimed. Known K4 letters/positions have no role in the solver, corpus, scorer, models, families, keys or seeds.
