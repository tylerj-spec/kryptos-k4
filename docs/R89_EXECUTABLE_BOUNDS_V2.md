# Proposed executable R89 v2: method-identification and strict-blind experiment bounds

Date: 2026-11-20. STATUS: DESIGN / EXECUTION SPECIFICATION ONLY. Not an immutable experiment preregistration; NO sealed gate and NO K4 trial has been executed under v2. This document supersedes the investigative recommendations, not the historical content, in `docs/R89_MULTI_FAMILY_EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md`. Requires independent review and fresh pre-commit before any new holdout is generated.

## Hypotheses eligible for the formal synthetic gate

- **Arm F26L (primary):** 26-letter keyed 2x13 fractionation followed by a K1/K2-style mixed-alphabet tableau. See `R89_ADVERSARIAL_METHOD_AUDIT.md` for reversible equations and reference tests. Exact nominal parameter count: 20 alphabet constructions * [4*3+6*5+8*7+10*9 = 188] pairs = 3760 inner settings, * [2*(10+8)=36] tableau key/sign/phase options = 135360 fully layered settings. A bare F26 comparator adds 3760 = 139120. Candidate generator ranks all 36 phases by IC of each candidate's inverted tableau stream, processes top 8 phases and bare control, meaning exactly 33840 full F26 language scores per ciphertext. The order, tie breaker, score, chosen generic word alphabet list, and top 8 are locked before holdout. Exclude any u==v for a full block. Include the full short final block and preserve all 97 characters.
- **Arm RCT (conditional secondary):** ciphertext-dependent irregular free-wiring rotor. 28 schedule tuples d in {-1,1}, m in {2,3,4,5}, q in 0..m-1 and 26! possible W, heuristically searched with 80 deterministic restarts * 16000 swap proposals per tuple; 28 * 80 * 16000 = 35,840,000 proposals per ciphertext, plus declared annealing/greedy refinements. Its generator is not an exhaustive test of the 26! wirings. References: R77/H8, R78 state machine, R79 slot rules. Each schedule must be checked for transition-map collisions/equivalence first. M1 is excluded if this audit finds the same schedule already tested under another name.
- **Negative/competitor generators:** standalone F26, old R87 regular rotor, R88 progressive Quagmire, periodic Quagmire/Vigenere, long-key running text, independent alphabet-substitution+transposition, plaintext autokey with controlled lag, and length-preserving ciphertext shuffles. At least five distinct families; a uniformly random 97-character key can only be a negative control, never an identified K4 algorithm.

DO NOT include the old broad M2 transposition-before-substitution guess, arbitrary two-free-alphabet OTP-type methods, unconstrained codebooks, or any key candidate selected from released K4 plaintext.

## Hard prerequisites before spending a fresh sealed control

1. Freeze the actual Python/C++ solver sources, reference encipherers, corpus and model SHA256 digests, general key list, tie breakers, all seeds and compiler flags. Validate F26 on a toy alphabet/coordinates and all final block lengths 1..11, including exceptional residue collisions; validate RCT on toy alphabets with state updates and altered plaintext/ciphertext errors.
2. Ensure no hidden source, public released words, published positions, masks, K4 candidate text or old postfreeze clue-selected parameters reach the solver sandbox. The K4 ciphertext itself is not part of synthetic development. The published K4 IC is for context only and must NOT be used to choose F26 keys, stage phases or acceptance cuts.
3. Reconcile R77 H8/R79 state-rule library against RCT's 28 transition tables; reconcile old routing experiments against rejected M2. Require an independent model/person to review and explicitly sign G0 method novelty, reversible formulas, and resource estimates.
4. Fix an end-to-end method classifier. Both solvers use the same frozen general 2/3/4-gram language model but optimize different size spaces and can overfit noise differently. Calibration must convert raw best scores into per-arm **empirical null-tail evidence or prespecified standardized units** using separate *development-only* negatives, then choose the maximum evidence over arms. The decision must not be changed after gate reveal. A forced classification MUST have an ABSTAIN option.
5. Synthetic positive and negative data must be drawn from freshly procured, license-compliant, SHA-pinned sources that were not used in prior R85/R86 development, source LM training, or any previous holdout. New text must not be accidentally revealed to the solver through file paths or side channels. Avoid text excerpts containing known K4 released phrases as a generic leakage tripwire.

## Sealed synthetic benchmark and preregistered GO/NO-GO

For each eligible arm, generate **48 new hidden-plaintext, hidden-key, hidden-family 97-letter messages** as follows:
- 12 per F26 block b=5/7/9/11, or 12 per RCT modulus m=2/3/4/5; cross every stratum with at least three nonoverlapping corpora/genres, balanced stage signs/keys and initial phase or m/q/d values. Use a 4x4 Latin-square-like schedule so no genre aligns with just one parameter difficulty.
- Genres: historical narrative/diary; formal historical nonfiction; and legally reusable modern news/technical English. Make a 4-parameter-strata × 3-genre grid with **4 samples in each cell**, giving 12 per mechanism stratum and 16 per genre. Exactly **12 of these 48 messages** use a separately frozen, length-preserving, Germanic-looking orthographic mask (one per cell); 36 are normally spelled. The ciphertext itself is uncorrupted for the 48 primary gate positives. All plaintext, methods and keys are hidden.
- Prepare **additional** robustness cases per family, **outside** the 48 gate positives: at least two independently re-encrypted plaintext-symbol corruptions, two ciphertext-symbol corruptions, one deliberate missed-state update for RCT, and every F26 final-block length. Report them without tuning or silently adding to the gate denominator. The 48 primary ciphertexts are not symbol-corrupted.
- At least **150 nonfamily/negative** messages from >=5 preselected confounder generators. Include at least 25 frequency-preserving English anagrams; alternate genre sources and phonetically altered text. Run the identical F26 stage shortlist and RCT full heuristic solver on every negative, with the same source hash and CPU budget.
- All candidates, keys, intermediate scores and process logs from the **48 positives per participating family plus 150 shared negatives** (up to **246 ciphertexts if both arms participate**) must be SHA256-frozen and **pushed before any concealed truth is opened**. Every item goes through the *same joint unknown-family search*; the independent checker reconstructs all 97 ciphertext letters.

A candidate arm earns a K4 trial only if ALL are true on this **new** gate:
- >= **42/48** positives for **each participating family** have the correct method and derived key (or a formally proven equivalent) and >=95/97 correct plaintext letters. Additionally require >= **9/12 in each mechanism-parameter stratum**, >= **12/16 in each genre**, and >= **8/12 in the predeclared orthographic-mask subset**. Report results separately for the remaining 36 standard-spelling positives.
- >= **36/48** of the 48 positives are 97/97 exact; for F26L, the true tableau stage is in the ciphertext-only phase shortlist for >= **46/48**. These bars are exploratory but fixed before the gate, not revised after failures.
- <= **1/150** wrong-family examples produces a **confident** false family declaration; all other negatives ABSTAIN. Confidence thresholds must be frozen from *development* data, not tuned on the sealed negatives.
- Independent forward encryptor checks every stored candidate, including 97th-letter boundary, phase, sign, actual derivation key and all state updates; no overflow or nondeterminism found.
- An incomplete, interrupted, memory-exceeding or timed-out job is **INCOMPLETE**, not NO_SIGNAL and not an exclusion. Do not touch K4 under a failed family gate.

A successful gate demonstrates capability for the *stated family and plaintext distributions*, NOT evidence that K4 uses that family. A family that fails must be retired from this K4 round even if a different family passes. Future improvement requires NEW pre-registration and completely new hidden holdout data; preserve the failed run untouched.

## Bounded CPU and memory budgets, fixed after pilot / before gate

The limited spent development speed measurements varied: the F26L phase-unknown pilot was ~46.8s/32 cases on one configuration (~1.5s per case). A later adversarial run was **interrupted by an execution-tool timeout after 22 of 56 inputs**; the elapsed time at interruption was **not established as 240 seconds**. Its partial results cannot calibrate the full negative-control ensemble. The RCT 80*16000*28 solve required ~109.8s/8 cases (~13.7s per case). **Do not represent optimistic pilot speed as a guaranteed timing.** Specify wall-clock timeout independently for each subprocess and fail-closed on any exceeded cap.

| Stage | Maximum proposed wall time | RAM cap | Complete outputs / search budget |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Family novelty and toy oracle pilot (source-only) | 1800 seconds/arm | 2048 MB | all enumerated toy settings and 3000 random 97-letter roundtrips |
| Complete unknown-family synthetic gate | 7200 seconds total on declared 4 CPU allocation | 4096 MB | 48 positives per eligible arm plus 150 negatives: **198** ciphertexts with one arm; **246** with both; each goes through both arms when both participate |
| K4 and exactly 200 frequency-preserving shuffles, conditional on GO | 7200 seconds total on same declared 4 CPUs | 4096 MB | 201 ciphertexts through every gate-passing arm, same seeds and full 28/33840 settings |
| Independent witnesses/score rescan and SHA freeze | 900 seconds | 2048 MB | all retained candidates, complete 97-letter reencryption |

If single-core runtime projection under the locked configuration exceeds 7200s, limit participating arms BEFORE creating the sealed gate; do not shorten the null set, alter cutoffs or reduce iterations after seeing positive or K4 results. Max retries **zero** within each formal stage; unexpected machine restarts are INCOMPLETE, recorded separately. Use only `audit/run_bounded.py` and the R53 read-only/no-network sandbox. Maximum retained full-text candidates: **top 5 per eligible arm per input** (e.g. <=2010 K4+null candidate entries if both pass); preserve all final per-setting score maxima/hashes to permit independent full-search comparisons without bloating commit size.

## Conditional K4 trial and strict outcome labels

K4 source is the unchanged original 97-letter ciphertext, checksum with one trailing newline SHA256 `2797de1451ab97118211735dcef370fc5acd70829f164aa043c47cbf40f0a5a3`. The input to the candidate generator is this ciphertext only, model and independently sourced parameters. Neither public published plaintext strings nor their positions can enter the runner, scores, prompts or key search. The 200 controls are deterministic Fisher-Yates permutations of the ciphertext frequencies with a seed frozen BEFORE K4 output evaluation. Additional distinct wrong-family ciphertexts remain a separate false-positive stratum, not incorrectly pooled with shuffles.

Identical bounded searches are run on K4 and each null using each eligible arm, with the same full parameter list and predeclared seed/schedule. Track both an arm-specific best and an **overall arm/parameter maximum**. For K4 and control j, compare the overall maximum normalized evidence statistic under the precommitted calibration. Family-wise `p=(1 + #{shuffled controls with best >= K4 overall best}) / 201`; require `p<=0.01` for a score anomaly (possible under 200 nulls), plus a completely unedited fluent 97-letter English message for a *linguistic prospect*. This is not a solution until an independently derived encryption key reproduces all ciphertext letters and a separate evaluator finds 24/24 published letters after all full outputs and parameters are frozen/pushed.

Chronological commits must be immutable and distinct: (A) method code/corpus manifests/preregistration; (B) holdout commitment and QA manifest; (C) sealed blind holdout output and PUSH; (D) holdout truth reveal and GO; (E) K4+200 null ciphertext-only inputs; (F) K4 candidates+keys+scores+witness+logs+FROZEN.sha256 and PUSH; (G) postfreeze published-letter evaluation; (H) independent review note and narrowly scoped ledger entry. Preregister method+key-source hypotheses BEFORE their unknown-family tests; avoid K4-derived route/key proposals.

Outcome labels: EXACT_SCOPE_EXCL only from a complete, independently reviewed exact proof whose assumptions cover the stated family; STAT_NO_SIGNAL for a powered blind search at null level; SOLVER_NO_POWER for a failed gate; SCORE_ANOMALY for a surprising but unreadable frozen decode; VERIFIED_BLIND_CANDIDATE only for independently reproducible full message/key/cipher that reenciphers exactly and independently passes 24/24. No external verifier submission without owner authorization. Do not overinterpret a self-consistent 97-letter fitted key.

## Decision table and explicit stop conditions

- RCT and F26L both fail unknown-family gate => no K4; report technical shortcomings and do not use published clues to repair them.
- Only F26L passes => K4+200 shuffles only through F26L; family-wise multiple-testing adjustment covers exactly that eligible arm. RCT remains synthetic development, never silently added.
- Only RCT passes => K4+200 shuffles only through RCT; the M1 subtype does NOT imply generic H8 is excluded on a null.
- Both pass => combined maximum normalized score per ciphertext under one global familywise null; never claim p based on the best individual arm in hindsight.
- Frozen K4 is score-anomalous but unreadable, or contradicts any released letter after freeze => rejected; no clue-fitting recovery or rerun allowed.
- A true complete derivation requires a second person/model, independent source reconstruction, 97/97 exact forward ciphertext and 24/24 later public-letter alignment; only then consider owner-approved outside answer check.

## Reproducibility and caveats

All pilot values come from previously spent R85/R86 samples and fixed R86 ngram model; they are for **method design only**. Crucially, the known ciphertext-only IC of K4 motivated trying an added tableau after the bare F26 proved statistically too structured. That is **post-hoc mechanism selection** and makes the synthetic IC resemblance descriptive, not confirmatory evidence; the later 200-shuffle p-value would be conditional on the selected family list, not correction for the entire exploratory history. The F26 development models were imported from the user's previously provided R85/R86 experiment archive; reproduction requires exact pinned bytes and a separately reviewed source release. Neither the K4 letters nor its published plaintext words were used to generate or rank candidate messages. A valid preregistration still needs an independent method-equivalence review, verified code checked into the repository, and source hashes for genuinely new corpora.

Historical references: WIRED Ed Scheidt 2005 (all four English, K4 masking technique not disclosed); ACA cipher catalog (rough plaintext lengths for hand methods); declassified 1943 U.S. Army Advanced Military Cryptography pp.58-59 (2x13 rectangle historically regarded impractical for conventional digraphic design). These are motivation and caveats, not evidence for any invented encryption recipe.
