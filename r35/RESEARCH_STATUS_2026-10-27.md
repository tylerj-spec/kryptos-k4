# R35 external research check (27 October 2026, Central)

## Public status and claims (not plaintext inputs)

- Public reporting and third-party research say the authentic K4 plaintext was discovered in the Smithsonian archival papers in 2025. This was **not** the cryptanalytic derivation of a mechanism. The authentic 97-letter text remains unpublished.
- A public reconstruction at https://solvekryptos.com/solution explicitly acknowledges it is *fitted* to the known 24 letters and that the rest is not independently confirmed. This does **not** satisfy this repository's strict-blind standard, and is **never to be ingested as a plant or decrypted candidate**.
- A competing research repository at https://github.com/nitsuah/kryptos/blob/main/docs/analysis/K4_ACTIVE_RESEARCH.md documents repeated crib position corrections and explores period-13 keystream interpretations. These are crib-assisted analyses, not evidence of a unique method.
- Continued search should prioritize independent out-of-sample prediction on planted messages and blind complete-language recovery. Contradictions or failures must be retained.

## Caveat on negative claims

A strict-blind setting-family scan failing all confirmed plaintext letters *excludes only the exact pre-registered family under the sampled key sources, route variants, alphabets, and operations*. It cannot exclude a different cipher architecture, an additional masking stage, a different edition of a book, or genuine misprints.

## Research priorities

1. Preserve the new successful R35 CI as a reproducible baseline.
2. Run the pre-registered R35 M1 mixed-direction rotation scan with planted controls, freeze, then separate reveal.
3. Build a two-free-alphabet **blind** solver that passes a large held-out planted-message gate before it sees K4.
4. Explicitly treat plausible reconstructed English from known letters as a warning about overfitting, not a result.

No K4 plaintext claimed, no external verifier queried, no post-hoc output substitutions.
